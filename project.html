SAT Revision Hub

Designer, Developer

Visit Website

Born from my achievement of a 1540 SAT score, The SAT Revision Hub represents my commitment to democratizing test preparation. Witnessing classmates struggle without adequate resources inspired me to transform my personal study materials into a comprehensive, accessible platform. What started as shared notes evolved into a full-fledged digital learning ecosystem, now helping students worldwide who lack financial means to access premium SAT prep resources.

Built entirely from scratch using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with AI-assisted development, this platform showcases my ability to identify real-world problems and craft practical solutions. The hub features meticulously organized study guides, practice questions, strategic approaches, and my personal techniques that led to my score (all freely available to anyone with internet access).

The Noders Project

Co-founder, Head of Content

Visit Website

The Noders emerged from a vision to ignite technological curiosity within my school community. As Co-founder and Head of Content, I spearheaded the creation of Vietnam's first student-led tech publication focusing on AI/ML and software engineering. Leading a passionate team of writers and creators, I've cultivated a culture of knowledge-sharing that transcends traditional classroom boundaries, inspiring peers to explore the cutting edge of computer science.

I develop comprehensive editorial strategies, mentor emerging tech writers, and ensure our articles maintain technical accuracy while remaining accessible. Through The Noders, we've published dozens of in-depth articles on machine learning algorithms, software development best practices, and emerging technologies, establishing ourselves as the go-to resource for tech-curious students at VNU-HCM High School for the Gifted.

Personal Portfolio Website

Designer, Developer

View Portfolio

This portfolio means more than just a collection of achievements, it's a digital narrative of my multifaceted journey as a student-athlete-developer. Meticulously crafted with modern web technologies, each section tells a story: from chess tournaments that taught me strategic thinking, to coding projects that showcase technical prowess, to community initiatives that reflect my values. The responsive, animation-rich design demonstrates my commitment to creating user experiences that are both aesthetically pleasing and functionally robust.

Built entirely with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, this portfolio showcases my mastery of front-end fundamentals without relying on frameworks. Every smooth animation, every responsive breakpoint, and every interactive element was hand-coded to create a seamless experience across all devices. The site balances professionalism with personality, reflecting my unique position at the intersection of mathematics, computer science, and chess.
