Chess Journey

Chess has been more than a game for me. It has been a two-year journey of persistence, growth, and leadership. From the moment I first discovered it to the time I began guiding others at Chess Nang Khieu, I have learned that every move, on and off the board, teaches patience, strategy, and the courage to help others grow with me.

June 2023

My First Chess Tournament

Where it all began — learning to stay calm under pressure and think several moves ahead. This first experience taught me patience, strategy, and the excitement of real competition.

My last tournament marked the end of a long journey — a test of focus, growth, and composure. It was both a challenge and a celebration of how much I had learned since my very first match.
