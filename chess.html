My Chess Journey

♟️ The Chess World That I Loved

The rhythmic click of chess clocks has always felt like home to me. Chess to me has been more than a passion; it has been an environment where I have grown and learned. From a chess player to the club's president, I have found where I belong and discovered my confidence. Now I know how to improvise when new "moves", whether on the chessboard or in my life, come unexpectedly.

Timeline of Milestones

June 2023

My First Chess Tournament

I was new, nervous, and had no idea what to expect. I learned how it felt to compete for real and how easily one bad move could ruin a game. This was the start of everything I later improved on.

Mar 2024

My Last Chess Tournament

I already had more experience and I understood the game better than before. This event showed me how much I had changed since my first match and it closed the chapter on my time competing in school.

June 2024

Chess Orientation Day

The start of a new season and new connections. Meeting teammates and learning our team's rhythm made me excited to keep improving and growing together.

Oct 2024

Chess Nang Khieu First Bonding

I was lucky to get to know my teammates beyond the board. My first presentation with the entire club, sharing my chess interests and future prospects with the club.

Oct 2024

Board Team Pictures

I started to feel part of a large community. Connecting with teammates and building a competitive space for everyone to compete was my dream in a long time.

Oct 2024

First Pictures Together

Our first full team photo, a start of something special. Standing side by side, I could feel the excitement and curiosity that brought us all together. It was the first moment we truly looked like a team.

Dec 2024

Chess Nang Khieu Christmas Open

I helped bring our first biggest event of the year to the stage. I saw everyone compete, laugh, and celebrate together. It was a proud moment that showed how far we had come as a team.

Dec 2024

Last Minute Check-in with Staff Team

Right after the tournament, we had some moments to take pictures of our team together. This was a mix of relief and pride and showed that we have made it happen.

May 2025

Chess Nang Khieu Rookie Tournament

Organizing my second big event taught me more than I expected, from managing schedules to cheering for every player's first win. Seeing others enjoy the tournament reminded me why I loved chess in the first place.

May 2025

Final Photos with the Team

By the time we took this picture, we had been through countless matches, meetings, and late nights. Behind every smile were inside jokes, shared stress, and quiet moments of pride.

May 2025

Handmade Gifts for Participation

We stayed up late preparing these handmade gifts; each one packed with effort and heartfelt notes. Those small gestures made the event feel warm and personal.

Jul 2025

Final Photos of the Year with My Bros

Our last photo of the school year felt bittersweet. We laughed about any kinds of jokes, helped each other on the chess journey. Looking back, I realized how this friendship had shaped my chess journey and who I'd become.

Aug 2025

Last Bonding Together

It was our final meetup before everyone started their new paths. We played, talked, and reflected on how far we'd come since that first day. This experience marked the ending of a significant chapter in my life, hoping to continue better in the future with new communities.
